I am an LLM researcher working on optimization and efficient training of neural networks. My current research includes low-rank pretraining, spectral regularization, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and reducing the memory cost of language-model training.

I study how optimization and regularization affect the singular-value spectra and effective rank of weight matrices. I am interested in spectral weight decay, nuclear-norm regularization, proximal methods, and Newton-Schulz approximations. A central question is whether training can make a model easier to compress with low-rank factorization while preserving its quality. Related questions concern generalization and memorization under label noise.

Another research direction is optimization for LoRA and other low-rank parameterizations. Relevant topics include Muon-style matrix updates, optimization of factorized weights, rebalancing, spectral constraints, and convergence analysis. I want to understand both the geometry of these updates and their practical behavior when fine-tuning language models.

I also work on memory-efficient LLM pretraining. Relevant methods include low-rank gradient and optimizer-state representations, GaLore, Frugal-style methods, and low-precision optimizer states such as FP8. I am interested in their tradeoffs between model quality, training memory, throughput, and communication cost, including how those tradeoffs change with model scale.

As a secondary current interest, I am building an AI research digest agent with a handwritten harness. Relevant work includes explicit context management, structured LLM decisions, application-controlled tools, and paper retrieval and relevance ranking using lexical or embedding-based methods.
