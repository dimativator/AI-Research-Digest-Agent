Interpretation and potential fixes:
The check correctly questioned AQL-Lite (gold0), but recommended the reasoning-search
survey (gold0) in every run. Runs1 and2 removed SAM (gold1). Run2 also removed
another gold1 and added a gold0.5. These are real selection failures, not mocked cases.
Likely contributors: the profile emphasizes building a research-digest harness;
the check sees600-character abstract excerpts and no inspected evidence. Its
argument that SAM lacks a direct noisy-label connection may reflect truncated
input, not the full abstract. Treat these as hypotheses to test.
Candidate fixes: reconcile profile priorities with the user, show full abstracts
for proposed replacements, and ask for stronger evidence before displacing a
strong candidate. Do not tune prompts using held-out gold labels as model input.
