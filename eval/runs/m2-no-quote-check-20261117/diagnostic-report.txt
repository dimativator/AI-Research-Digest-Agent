DIAGNOSIS AND VARIANT IDEAS
1. Removing final exact matching eliminated the observed finish-repair loop in both completed repetitions: each finished on its first finish action. This establishes completion under the modified validator, not factual accuracy of summaries or quotes. Exact quotations are no longer mechanically guaranteed.

2. Screening can still abort the entire run. Run3 trace lines 4, 7, 10 repeat the same unsupported quote for NoRA (2608.31036). This is three consecutive failures despite paper/quote-specific feedback. Unlike the final loop, the three-attempt screening cap terminates the run. Potential common repair: evidence by source ID, or failure isolation per paper with explicit uncertainty rather than aborting the whole batch. Evaluate separately; no change applied here.

3. Personal-interest selection is unstable. Run1 selected gold-0 papers Signals and Queries (2610.29326) and Reasoning-Search-Augmented LLMs (2610.29189). Run2 replaced them with gold-1 2609.40127 and Context Language Models (2609.37725). Both share three gold-1 papers: spectral weight decay, approximate Muon with adapters, and NoRA. The sets overlap on 3/5 entries, Jaccard 3/7. A selection critic could compare broad agent-related matches with stronger unselected optimization papers. This is a hypothesis; no critic evaluation yet.

4. Prompt-induced over-investigation persists. Run1 trace line19 calls spectral weight decay the strongest direct match, then asks for baselines and label-noise controls. Run2 line16 does the same. All seven tool calls across completed runs are inspections. No find_code, search_candidates, or expand_shortlist calls. A selective-investigation variant could require a concrete personal-relevance ambiguity before inspecting, reducing calls and context growth. It may still lose useful disambiguation; test against abstract-only selection.

5. Earlier annotations can anchor later decisions. Both completed screenings call four gold-0 papers relevant (RIFT, AQL-Lite, Signals and Queries, reasoning-search survey). Initial shortlist contains 10 gold-1, four gold-0.5, sixteen gold-0. All ten gold-1 were screened relevant in both successful runs. Initial coverage is limited but enough high-rated candidates exist for five recommendations. Compare draft selection with and without the earlier screening labels as a future controlled ablation.

6. Source text remains imperfect. Fixture passages retain duplicate math/LaTeX representations. The validator change allows completion but does not repair extraction. Source normalization and manual summary-support checks remain separate work.

LIMITATIONS
Three repetitions of a single corpus task are not 300 independent tasks. There is no same-protocol one-call LLM baseline run here. Labels evaluate interest, not redundancy, scientific truth, or summary faithfulness. Original M2 20-paper evaluation was not rerun. Tool fixtures were captured on first use, not all frozen before the original diagnostic; future comparisons must reuse these files and record any new fixture capture. Input snippets are truncated in the production decision context. Exact model snapshot/reasoning effort is not independently pinned beyond model name and recorded CLI version.

RECOMMENDED NEXT EXPERIMENT
Fix screening failure handling as shared infrastructure, retaining this real failed trace. Compare corrected ReAct, abstract-only selector, and selection critic on identical inputs/fixtures. Test selective investigation afterward to isolate its effect. Keep BM25 top5 as a cheap baseline. Do not tune the profile using hidden evaluation labels and then present the same sample as untouched validation.
